Odoo 18 records an opening cash difference in the point of sale session
chatter, but does not create the corresponding accounting entry.

This module restores the accounting correction for future sessions.
Opening cash gains and losses are posted in the cash journal against its
configured profit or loss account.

The correction is linked to the session for audit purposes and appears
in its related journal items. It is not linked as a session cash
movement, which prevents Odoo 18 from including it again in the
theoretical closing balance.

Configuration
=============

On the cash journal used by the point of sale, configure both a **Profit
Account** and a **Loss Account**.

These accounts are used for positive and negative opening cash
differences, respectively. A missing account blocks the session opening
when that type of difference is detected.
